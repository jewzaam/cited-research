# Game Overview & Early-Access State

Context for the rest of this research. See [citations](../citations.md) for sources.

## Identity

- **Developer / Publisher:** Creepy Jar [1]
- **Platform:** PC (Steam), App ID 1631270 [1]
- **Working title prior to launch:** Chimera (per Discovery agent snippet; not
  verified in a fetched source)
- **Early Access launch:** January 6, 2026 [1]
- **Planned 1.0 window:** 2027 — Creepy Jar states the game will "stay in Early
  Access for about a year" [1]
- **Current price:** $15.99 at research time (2026-04-17), discounted 20% from
  $19.99 [1]

## Genre & Core Loop

- Genre tags on Steam: Action, Adventure, Indie, Simulation, Early Access [1]
- Description: first-person open-world base-building game combining exploration,
  resource management, industrial automation, and combat against alien creatures
  for solo or up-to-4-player co-op [1]
- Setting: planet Arcadia-7 (named across multiple Discovery-agent sources;
  corroborated by [22])
- Core loop observed by reviewers: mining → smelting → shipping ([27] calls the
  loop "works well"); interrupted periodically by "Rupture" events (see
  [Patch Volatility](patch-volatility.md) and
  [Early-Game Optimization](early-game-optimization.md))

## Reception

- Overall Steam reviews: 82% positive, 5,858 English reviews ("Very Positive") [1]
- Recent 30-day Steam reviews: 77% positive, 1,155 reviews ("Mostly Positive") [1]
- The 5-point gap between overall and recent suggests modest sentiment decline in
  the Update-1 launch window, but the 30-day score remains positive. Treat the
  specific percentages as snapshot values for 2026-04-17.
- Single fetched critic review: NeonLightsMedia 7.0/10 — "A stunningly beautiful
  mess that needs a few more months in the oven" [27]
- Two other critic reviews (Screen Hype, The Review Geek) were identified but
  returned 403 during fetch [38][39]. Discovery-agent snippet from [39] quotes
  "wide as an ocean and deep as a puddle"; treat as unverified.

## Player-Count Context (Unverified)

Massively Overpowered [8] reportedly documented a decline from a January EA-launch

indicative-but-unconfirmed; do not rely on the specific percentage for decisions.

## Why the EA State Matters for Progression Optimization

- **Stability window:** The 2026-04-17 snapshot sits 8 days after Update 1
  shipped (April 9) and 1 day after Hotfix 0.2.1 (April 16). Guidance produced
  during this window is meaningfully more stable than pre-Update-1 guidance but
  less stable than mid-cycle EA guidance would be. See
  [Patch Volatility](patch-volatility.md) for version-specific staleness flags.
- **Endgame gap:** Per community consensus [33] and developer statements
  recovered via [19], a "definitive endgame" is a 1.0 target. Current terminal
  objective is the World Engine / Forgotten Engine activation plus tech
  teleporter unlock. This directly shapes what "optimal progression" can mean
  today — there is no true speedrun target beyond the current content ceiling.
