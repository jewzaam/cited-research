# Early-Game Optimization

First ~5 hours, solo/duo first-playthrough. See [citations](../citations.md) for sources.

## Immediate Spawn Actions

1. **Loot the Orbital Lander Personal Storage boxes.** "Check both storage
   containers, and you will get 20 of them [starter rations]. They will get
   you through the first few hours of the game with no worries." [20]
2. **Read the Mainframe** inside the lander for a mission primer (from
   Discovery-agent snippets across multiple beginner guides).
3. **Place your Base Core** to establish the oxygen bubble and build zone
   (mentioned in multiple Discovery snippets; see [Progression Systems
   Inventory](progression-systems-inventory.md)).

## Survival Meters (Arcadia-7 environment)

Three meters, per Discovery-agent consensus across beginner guides:

- **Calories:** Polifruit (common, available wherever grass grows) provides
  calories without toxicity [21]
- **Hydration:** Hydrobulb (near water) restores hydration without toxicity [21]
- **Toxicity:** Purplant reduces toxicity instantly [21]

Per [30]: "Hydration + Calories first. If those aren't stable, everything else
feels harder than it needs to be."

Per [20]: Food Station does not unlock until Future Health Solutions Level 6,
so early-game food is forage-based.

## Corporation Unlock Order

Two sources give consistent guidance: bring all corporations to Level 2 cheaply
first, then focus [25][29]. Divergence on what to prioritize at Level 3:

**TheGamer order [14][13]:**
1. Moon Energy L3 — Map (Fog of War removal)
2. Selenian L2 — Fabricator
3. Griffits Blue L2 — UPP-7 Reaper Pistol
4. Clever Robotics L2 — Personal Storage (18 slots)
5. Future Health Solutions L3 — Regeneration Chamber, Medtool
6. Selenian L3 — +8 character inventory slots

**starrupture.net four-phase order [29]:**
1. Moon Energy L3
2. Selenian L2
3. Future Health Solutions L6 (Food Station — this is a longer-term target)
4. Griffits Blue L2

Both sources agree that **Moon Energy L3 (Map) is the first priority** [25][14][29].
The Map removes Fog of War and is a "must-have" for planning routes [25].

**Critical Update-1 caveat:** Update 1 (April 9, 2026) reset all corporation
levels to L1 and converted prior progress to Data Points [2][42]. Any pre-April-9
guide frames progression against an older state — if you loaded a pre-Update-1
save, your levels were reset. Post-Update-1 fresh saves are unaffected.

## Data Point Strategy (Critical Early-Game Trap)

**Do not spend Data Points on corporation leveling.** [20]:

> "Datapoints are essential for unlocking various recipes and materials, so
> spending them on leveling can be a waste. Make sure to save them and use
> them in the Recipe Station instead."

Primary DP yields [10][11]:

- **5x Ignitium bundle = 2,000 Data Points** [11] — only available post-Rupture,
  disintegrates quickly
- **Broken technology pieces = 400–2,000 DP** by quality, from Fallen Drones,
  Former Colonist bodies, ClayWood Corporation survey ruins [11]
- **Glowcap = 90 DP/piece** [10] — in underground caves, post-wave only
- **Rare plants (Purplants, Serpent Root, Star Tears) = 20+ DP each** [10]
- **Common plants (Polifruit, Hydrobulb, Prickler) = 5 DP each** [10]

**Never analyze Meteor Hearts** — they are used for Base Cores and shelter
building; analyzing them destroys progression-critical components [20].

**Quartz ore cannot be analyzed** at all [10].

## Rupture Mechanics

The "Rupture" is a periodic stellar flare event — not a wave of enemies, though
waves are a separate recurring hazard. Key mechanics:

- **Countdown warning:** "You'll get a countdown for the wave, which only lasts
  for 15 seconds" [13]
- **Outcome:** Instant death if caught outside shelter; surface incineration;
  some plants (Glowcap, Oxallop) and Ignitium spawn only in the post-Rupture
  window [10][11][21]

### Pocket Base Kit (Emergency Shelter)

Multiple Discovery sources document a "Pocket Base" strategy: carry 1 Meteor
Heart + ~100 Basic Building Materials to drop an emergency Base Core + Habitat
anywhere on the map. Materials refunded when deconstructed. This strategy
appeared in Discovery-agent summaries but was not recovered verbatim from a
single fetched source — treat the 100 BBM figure as indicative rather than
exact.

## Post-Rupture Farming Window

Counter-Discovery surfaced a consensus that the post-Rupture window is the
optimal scavenging period, not a "hide and wait" period. The intuitive framing
("survive, then wait for calm") inverts the actual opportunity:

- **Ignitium** (highest DP yield) spawns only post-Rupture and disintegrates
  rapidly [11]
- **Glowcap** (90 DP/piece) only harvestable in underground caves post-wave [10][21]
- **Oxallop** only harvestable between post-wave and regeneration [21]
- **Sulfur Extractors** must be placed just after a rupture when temperatures
  drop [22]

## Beginner Traps (Documented)

1. **Analyzing Meteor Hearts** for Data Points [20]
2. **Spending Data Points on contract acceleration** before exhausting recipe
   research [20]
3. **Leaving base without setting a Commission** on the OCL (wastes passive
   progression time) [12]
4. **Using only one OCL** for all commissions — multiple OCLs run in parallel,
   speeding up 6,400-point deliveries [20]
5. **Building at the landing pod** — resources needed later aren't there [12];
   recommended move is north of the lake east of starting area [12]
6. **Overextending on exploration** without weapons [20]; crafted Pistol first,
   "over 500" rounds per expedition [13]
7. **Not climbing high ground** when placing buildings — "Height will greatly
   alter the perspective while placing buildings in StarRupture" [12]
8. **Upgrading the Base Core early** — triggers escalating enemy waves from
   nearby monoliths and is irreversible [31]
9. **Building push-forward production lines** — the game uses pull-based
   logistics; items move only when requested downstream, and circular loops
   jam the whole network [31]

## Safe-to-Defer Early

- Character choice (cosmetic) [13]
- Base aesthetics — functional over architectural (from Discovery-agent
  consensus)
- Advanced corporation tiers (L4+) before unlocking the Map at Moon Energy L3

## Min-Max / Multiplayer Callouts

Per user-selected archetype (solo/duo first-playthrough), min-max and
multiplayer divergences are called out only when they materially change a
recommendation:

- **Multiplayer:** Up to 4 players via host-joined world; PvE only; no public
  matchmaking [49]. Corporation progress is shared on host world. No other
  divergences recommended; same priority order applies.
- **Min-max:** Experienced players skip the "all corps to L2 first" step and
  focus directly on a single-corporation rush (Selenian typically, for
  Fabricator and later Helium-3). No fetched source documents a full
  speedrun meta; this is Discovery-level consensus.
