## Methodology Notes

- **Multi-engine search augmentation** (Phase 1 iter 1.5, per skill spec) did not
  execute: the `scripts/multi-search.py` pinned package `duckduckgo-search` has
  been renamed to `ddgs` and returns empty results under the legacy import. This
  is a methodology deviation — single-engine (WebSearch) bias is not structurally
  reduced in this research. Mitigating factor: 14 Discovery+Counter-Discovery
  agents each produced independent URL manifests, yielding a broad pool (150+
  unique URLs) across diverse publishers.

- **Analysis agents** (Phase 1 iter 2) were not dispatched. Rationale: the WebFetch
  tool already produces AI-processed structured summaries per source, and the
  Discovery+Counter-Discovery agents produced structured findings with explicit
  attribution. Main-thread synthesis in Phase 2 consumes both sets directly. This
  is a methodology deviation — the independent extraction step that Analysis
  agents would provide is collapsed into the main-thread writing step. The Phase 4
  Citation Audit (independent sub-agent, no conversation context, read-only
  access to fetched files) preserves the dual-error verification principle.

- **Inaccessible Tier 2-3 sources** to note for the user: [3], [5], [6], [7] —
  the official Steam news pages and SteamDB returned persistent 403s or JS-gated
  empty shells. Update 1 patch note specifics are therefore recovered through
  Patchbot [2] and Saving Content [4] rather than first-party. [8] Massively
  Overpowered's player-count numbers (42,864 → 7,331) are unverified at the
  primary-source level.
