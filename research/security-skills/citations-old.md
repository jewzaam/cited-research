# Citations — AI-Generated Test Trust

All sources visited in-session via WebSearch on 2026-03-27.

**Data quality caveat:** WebFetch permission was denied during this research
session. All data below was extracted from WebSearch result summaries, not from
direct page visits.

## [1] Liu et al. — EvalPlus (NeurIPS 2023)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01210
- **Data:** HumanEval+ augments HumanEval by 80x tests. pass@k reduced 19.3-28.9%.

## [2] Dakhama et al. — MuTAP (IST 2024)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16557
- **Data:** 93.57% mutation score. HumanEval: 94% vs Pynguin 66%.

## [3] Inozemtseva & Holmes (ICSE 2014)
- **URL:** https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rtholmes/papers/icse_2014_inozemtseva.pdf
- **Data:** Low-moderate coverage-effectiveness correlation controlling for size.

## [4] Chen et al. (ASE 2020)
- **URL:** https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~rjust/publ/mutants_faults_revisited_ase_2020.pdf
- **Data:** Statement coverage detects 10% of faults. Mutation score superior.
