## Gaps and Limitations

- **YouTube video IDs not retrievable**: YouTube channel pages render via JavaScript and do not expose watch URLs to web fetchers. Channel handles and content topics are confirmed through secondary sources, but specific video IDs could not be verified for Deep Space Astro, most Nico Carver videos, and Cuiv the Lazy Geek [81][85][86].
- **Nico Carver specific Siril tutorials**: His channel is confirmed to cover Siril extensively, but individual video titles and URLs for Siril-specific content beyond the M31/M42 processing comparisons were not enumerable [81][82][83][84].
- **Paywall uncertainty**: Some Telescope.Live tutorials may require subscription; only those marked "[Free]" are confirmed free [49].
- **GraXpert official channel**: @astrophotocologne is referenced on graxpert.com but no direct channel URL was confirmed [93].
