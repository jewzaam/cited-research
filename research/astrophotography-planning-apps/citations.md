# Citations — Astrophotography Planning Apps & Gap Landscape

All numbered sources cited from the reference files and main analysis. Tier rubric:
- **T1**: peer-reviewed, government, or institutional research
- **T2**: vendor official site, established reference site, App Store / Play Store official listings
- **T3**: industry blog, established reviewer, trade publication

Date column reflects the page's last-confirmed-fetch date during this research (April 2026) unless an explicit publication year is shown in the source.
