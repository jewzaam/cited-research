**Source:** <https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14720v1>

**Claims checked:**
- Three techniques (delimiting, datamarking, encoding) — **Confirmed**
- Datamarking reduces ASR "from ~50% to <3%" on GPT-3.5-Turbo — **Imprecise**. Source says 3.10% for document summarization, 8.0% for document Q&A. The "<3%" claim is only accurate for one task type. The deliverable states "Datamarking reduces ASR from ~50% to <3% on GPT-3.5-Turbo" which does not reflect the 8.0% Q&A result.
- Encoding achieves 0% ASR on GPT-4 — **Partially confirmed**. 0% for summarization; varies by task.
- No impact on SQuAD/SuperGLUE/IMDB — **Confirmed**
- Datamarking replaces whitespace — **Confirmed**

**Impact:** The "<3%" claim appears in the main deliverable (prompt-injection-defenses.md) and README summary table. The actual range is 3.10%-8.0% depending on task, making the headline claim optimistic.
