### [1] MDA Framework — Eight Aesthetics

**Claim in documents:** Eight aesthetic categories: Sensation, Fantasy, Narrative, Challenge, Fellowship, Discovery, Expression, Submission. Designer-side runs Mechanics → Dynamics → Aesthetics; player-side experiences Aesthetics ← Dynamics ← Mechanics. Developed at GDC workshops 2001–2004.

**Pre-fetched file:** `mda-framework.md`

**Source text:**
> Eight Aesthetic Categories: 1. Sensation: Pleasure from sensory stimulus. 2. Fantasy: Immersion in alternative realities. 3. Narrative: Engagement through storytelling. 4. Challenge: Satisfaction from overcoming obstacles. 5. Fellowship: Social connection with other players. 6. Discovery: Joy of exploration and learning. 7. Expression: Self-representation and creativity. 8. Submission: Relaxation through engaging escapism.
>
> Designers craft mechanics, while players experience aesthetics. Dynamics function as the interpretive bridge between designer intent and player experience. Developed through GDC workshops spanning 2001–2004.

**Grade: VERIFIED**

All eight aesthetics listed in exact order and labeling. M→D→A direction and the asymmetric designer/player perspective are confirmed. Development timeline confirmed.
