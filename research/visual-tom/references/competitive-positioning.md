## Analyst Positioning

### 2024 Gartner Magic Quadrant for SOAP

Absyss positioned as **Niche Player** in the 2024 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Service Orchestration and Automation Platforms, published September 11, 2024. 13 vendors evaluated [16].

| Quadrant | Vendors |
|---|---|
| Leaders | Redwood Software (RunMyJobs), Stonebranch (UAC) |
| Challengers | Rocket Software |
| Visionaries | Beta Systems Software (ANOW!) |
| Niche Players | SMA Technologies (OpCon), **Absyss (Visual TOM)**, Honico Systems (BatchMan) |

Gartner defines Niche Players as vendors that "focus successfully on a small segment or are unfocused and do not out-innovate or outperform others" [16].

### 2025 EMA Radar for Workload Automation

Absyss included among 10 vendors meeting "enterprise-grade execution and visible innovation momentum" criteria in the 2025 EMA Radar, published October 31, 2025, by analyst Dan Twing [17].

The 10 vendors: Absyss, Arvato Systems, Beta Systems, BMC, Broadcom, HCLSoftware, IBM, Redwood Software, Rocket Software, Stonebranch [17].

EMA identified "a structural shift in the market, with orchestration now defined by three converging innovation domains: agentic AI, observability-enabled automation, and modernized orchestration architectures" [17].

⚠ The specific scoring and positioning of Absyss within the EMA Radar is behind a $1,495 paywall and was not accessible [17].

### Gartner Peer Insights

Visual TOM rated 4.9/5.0 on Gartner Peer Insights with a Net Promoter Score of 94 [25]. ⚠ Sample size is reportedly small (9 reviews at time of research), which limits the statistical significance of these ratings.
