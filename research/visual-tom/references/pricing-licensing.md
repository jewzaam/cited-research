# Pricing & Licensing

Dimension covering Visual TOM's licensing models, editions, measurement criteria, and commercial positioning. See [citations.md](../citations.md) for full source details.

## Licensing Models

Visual TOM offers three acquisition models [2]:

| Model | Description | Support/Maintenance |
|---|---|---|
| Perpetual | Own the licenses outright | Annual maintenance at 20% of license value [2] |
| Subscription | Annual subscription (on-premises) | Included [2] |
| SaaS | Cloud-based via partner channels | Included [2] |

## Editions

Three packaged editions, each offering a different level of features [1][2]:

| Edition | Target |
|---|---|
| Starter | Entry-level |
| Performance | Mid-tier |
| Ultimate | Full-featured |

Specific feature differentiation between editions is not publicly documented. The functional differential document (v7.2) was inaccessible [27].

## Measurement Criteria

Customers choose their pricing metric [1][2]:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Agents | Number of Visual TOM agents deployed |
| Executions | Number of daily job executions via execution packs |
| Processes | Number of processes managed |

Execution packs set "the legal limit of usage (but not the operational limit)" [2], meaning contracted capacity is a licensing boundary rather than a technical ceiling.

## Pricing Transparency

Absyss does not publish pricing figures. The pricing page directs to a contact form for custom quotes [2]. Third-party review sites (Capterra, SoftwareWorld) report pricing as "Not provided by vendor."

## 20% Maintenance Rate

The perpetual license maintenance rate of 20% annually [2] falls within the typical enterprise software range of 18-22%. This covers support and software updates.

## TCO Claims

Absyss claims migrations generate "strong ROI by lowering licensing/maintenance as well as operating costs" [8]. A specific "30-50% TCO savings" figure appears in marketing brochure search snippets [26], but the brochure PDF was inaccessible for verification. The methodology behind any TCO calculation is not published.

## Partner Pricing

- Three partner levels: ACTIVE, DYNAMIC, STRATEGIC [5]
- "Adapted pricing policy with competitive replacement offer" for migrations from competing products [8]
- SaaS model explicitly delivered through partners rather than direct from Absyss [2]

## Free Trial

Free trial available without credit card requirement. Trial duration not specified publicly.

## Competitive Pricing Context

Visual TOM's three-metric flexibility (Agents/Executions/Processes) is unusual — most competitors standardize on one or two metrics. The modular approach allows customers to optimize costs based on their deployment pattern [2].

## Gaps and Limitations

- No public pricing figures exist for any edition or licensing model
- Feature differentiation between Starter, Performance, and Ultimate editions is not publicly documented
- Execution pack pricing and volume discounts are not disclosed
- SaaS vs. on-premises cost differential is not available
- Geographic pricing variations are not documented
- The specific "30-50% TCO savings" figure from brochure snippets [26] could not be verified (PDF inaccessible); the migration page [8] claims ROI without quantifying a percentage
- Trial duration is not specified
