# Visual TOM from Absyss

Visual TOM is a workload automation and job scheduling platform from Absyss, a French software publisher (founded 1990, €13.4M revenue, 20-49 employees). It provides cross-platform orchestration from mainframes (z/OS, IBM i, GCOS, VMS) through cloud and containers (Docker, Kubernetes, Azure, AWS, GCP). Positioned as a **Niche Player** in the 2024 Gartner Magic Quadrant for SOAP and included in the 2025 EMA Radar (10 vendors).

## Quick Decision Framework

1. **Do you need mainframe + cloud in one scheduler?** Visual TOM covers z/OS, IBM i, GCOS, VMS alongside Docker/K8s/cloud — few competitors match this breadth.

2. **Is your primary market France/Europe?** Absyss's customer base, partner network, and support are strongest in France and French-speaking regions. The MFT product is positioned as a French sovereign alternative to US vendors.

3. **Can you accept a Niche Player vendor?** Absyss is profitable and established (35 years) but small (20-49 employees). Evaluate support capacity for your deployment scale.

4. **Do you need public compliance certifications?** No ISO 27001, SOC 2, or GDPR certifications were found in public sources. Clarify with Absyss directly.

5. **Are you migrating from Control-M, Dollar Universe, OpCon, or Automator?** Absyss has a proven migration track record (30+ migrations, 1.5M+ jobs transferred) with dedicated tooling.
